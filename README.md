# MTEL 278 Math Arcade

Browser games for practicing the math on the **MTEL 278** test (Mathematics, Science, and Technology/Engineering), Objectives 011–015.

## ▶ Play: https://jlgagnon-glitch.github.io/MTEL-Math-Practice/

It runs in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. You don't need to install anything. You can also download `index.html` and open it directly.

## The games

| Game | Objective | What you practice |
|---|---|---|
| Factor Forge | 011 | Prime factorization, GCF and LCM, divisibility rules |
| Number Sense Sprint | 011 | A 75-second timed round: place value, order of operations, integers, exponents, scientific notation, rational and irrational numbers, rounding, estimation |
| Number Line Dash | 012 | Placing fractions, decimals and percents on a number line; ratios, percents and unit rates |
| Function Machine | 013 | Finding function rules, patterns and sequences, slope and intercept, equations and inequalities, systems, direct and inverse variation, the meaning of the equal sign |
| Shape Lab | 014 | Angles, polygons, area and perimeter, the Pythagorean theorem, circles, volume and scaling, transformations, symmetry, similar figures |
| Data Detective | 015 | Mean, median, mode and range; box plots; probability; counting; circle graphs; elapsed time; unit and temperature conversions; choosing a graph |
| Misconception Detective | All | Finding the error in student work and choosing the best teaching activity |

## How it works

- The questions are new every time you play.
- Every answer comes with a step-by-step explanation.
- At the end of a game you get a score and 0–3 stars, and you can review every question you missed.
- Best scores are saved in your browser.

*These are original practice questions. They do not come from the test maker and are not official MTEL materials.*
